How it works

The same simulator used for every other policy.

What it sees: how full the inventory position is compared with the forecast target (8 levels) and whether recent demand ran above or below the forecast (3 levels).

What it can do: order up to the forecast target, 15% less, or 15% more.

What it is rewarded for: low holding cost and few lost sales each day.

How it learns: tabular Q-learning on windows of the training period only. The test days are never used for learning.
